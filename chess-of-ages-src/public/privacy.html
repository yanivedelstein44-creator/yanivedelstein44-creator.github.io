Privacy Policy

Chess of Ages: Island Realms · Last updated: 2026-11-12

The short version

  • No account, no cookies, no ads, no analytics and no tracking pixels.
  • Your islands, museum, gems, outfits, pets, board skins and settings are stored only on your device. We can't see them.
  • Games against the bots, the Time Pirates or a friend on the same device are played entirely on your device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.
  • Online play with a friend is optional. Only what that game needs (a random anonymous ID, the display name you chose, your avatar's look and your moves) goes through Google Firebase. Only people who have your private room code can see it. No strangers, no free-text chat.
  • Purchases are optional. In the Google Play version, Google Play handles payment and we never see your payment details. The website and test versions use a test shop that never charges money.
  • You can erase everything at any time: Settings → Delete all my data.

3. Online play with a friend (optional)

What is sent. When you create or join a private room, the game sends: a random anonymous user ID, the display name you chose, your avatar's outfit, pet and symbol, the room code, your color, the moves, draw and rematch requests, the fixed reactions you send (there is no free-text chat) and whether you're connected.

Why. Only to run the online game you asked for. For users in the EU and UK, the legal basis is providing the service you requested (GDPR article 6(1)(b)).

Who processes it. Google Firebase (Realtime Database, with the data stored in Belgium in the EU, and Anonymous Authentication) runs online play for us as our service provider. Like any internet service, Google also processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver it. Google may process technical and sign-in data in other countries under its data processing terms.

Who can see it. Only people who have the room code: the friend you invited and spectators you share the code with. There is no matchmaking with strangers, and rooms can't be listed or searched.

How long. A room is deleted when the player who created it leaves it, or the next time that player opens online play if an old room was left open. Settings → Delete all my data also deletes the rooms you created from the server.

Is it required? No law requires you to give this data, and online play is optional. If you don't want to send it, don't use online play. Everything else in the game works without it.

4. Sharing and who receives data

We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't use it for advertising. Data reaches others only in these cases:

  • When you share. Invite links, room codes and share images are created on your device and sent by you, through WhatsApp or your device's share menu. We don't receive the recipients' details.
  • Service providers. Google Firebase for online play (section 3), GitHub, which hosts the website and the updates (section 5), and Google Play for installing the app and for payments (section 6). Each processes data under its own terms.
  • When the law requires it. For example, a valid order from a competent authority.

5. Website, hosting and automatic updates

The same game is released through two channels:

  • Our website and the Android app downloaded from it: the website, the game's files and its updates are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.).
  • The Google Play version: Google Play installs and updates the app under Google's privacy policy. The game's content updates inside the app are also downloaded from GitHub Pages, from a separate folder for that version.

As a hosting provider, GitHub may log technical data when files are fetched, such as IP address, device and browser type, and request time. It uses that data to deliver the files and keep the service secure.

When the game opens, and then about every 20–30 minutes while it's open, it checks a small version.json file for its channel and installs updates at a safe moment. No usage statistics are sent with these checks.

6. Purchases

Islands 1–99 are free, and the game has no ads at all. These optional items cost real money:

  • Premium Islands: a one-time purchase that unlocks islands 100–129 and the Golden Clockwork Dragon.
  • Double gems for a week: for 7 days, every win against a bot or the Time Pirates gives twice the gems. Each purchase adds another 7 days.
  • Board skins: cosmetic looks for the board and pieces, each a one-time purchase. Other skins are free and earned by conquering islands.
  • Legendary Sky Nest pets (Thunder Dragon, Crystal Griffin, Sky Whale): cosmetic flying pets, each a one-time purchase. The other Sky Nest pets are earned by playing.

Gems are never sold. You earn them by playing. Nothing that is sold makes the bots weaker or helps you win a game. Free rewards such as daily gifts and the Royal Treasure Chest are never sold for money.

Payment. In the Google Play version, Google Play processes payments under Google's terms and privacy policy. We receive only a confirmation of the purchase (product, time and an order reference, with no payment details). That confirmation is stored on your device together with the rest of your progress. On the website and in test versions the shop runs in test mode: no money is charged and no payment details are collected. Refunds are explained in the Terms of use.

7. Children

The game doesn't ask for an email, phone number or real name. Please choose a display name that isn't your full real name. Online play only connects you with a friend you invite using a private code: there's no matchmaking with strangers and no free-text chat, only fixed reactions. A parent or guardian who has a question about a child's use of the game, or wants a child's data deleted, can contact us at the address in section 1.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete all my data erases your progress and items on this device, deletes the online rooms you created and removes the anonymous sign-in. You can also clear the site's data or uninstall the app.
  • Your rights under the law: depending on where you live, you may have the right to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to its use. This includes Israel's Privacy Protection Law and the EU/UK GDPR, which also give the rights to restrict processing and to data portability. Write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. Because almost everything is stored only on your device, we usually hold no data about you outside an active online room.
  • Complaints: you may complain to the Israeli Privacy Protection Authority, or to the data protection authority where you live.

9. Security

The website, updates and online play use HTTPS. Online rooms are protected by server-side security rules: rooms can't be listed, only people with the code can open a room, only the two players can change the game, and only the player whose turn it is can make a move. We collect as little as the game needs.

10. Changes

If we change how the game handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. A significant change will also be shown in the game.

  • הזכויות שלך לפי החוק: לפי חוק הגנת הפרטיות בישראל, ולפי ה-GDPR באיחוד האירופי ובבריטניה, ייתכן שיש לך זכות לעיין במידע עליך, לתקן אותו, למחוק אותו או להתנגד לשימוש בו. ה-GDPR נותן גם זכות להגביל עיבוד ולקבל את המידע בפורמט נייד. כתוב אל [CONTACT EMAIL]. כמעט הכול נשמר רק במכשיר שלך, ולכן בדרך כלל אין אצלנו מידע עליך מחוץ לחדר אונליין פעיל.
  • תלונות: אפשר להגיש תלונה לרשות להגנת הפרטיות, או לרשות להגנת המידע במדינה שבה אתה גר.

9. אבטחה

האתר, העדכונים והמשחק אונליין פועלים ב-HTTPS. חדרי האונליין מוגנים בכללי אבטחה בצד השרת: אי אפשר לראות רשימת חדרים, רק מי שיש לו את הקוד יכול לפתוח חדר, רק שני השחקנים יכולים לשנות את המשחק, ורק השחקן שתורו יכול לבצע מהלך. אנחנו אוספים רק את המינימום שהמשחק צריך.

10. שינויים

אם נשנה את הדרך שבה המשחק מטפל במידע, נעדכן את הדף הזה ואת התאריך שבראשו לפני שהשינוי ייכנס לתוקף. על שינוי משמעותי נודיע גם בתוך המשחק.
