Privacy Policy & Accessibility

Chess of Ages: Kingdom Builder · Last updated: November 6, 2026

The short version

  • No account, no cookies, no ads, no analytics and no tracking pixels.
  • Your progress, gems, outfits and settings are stored only on your device. We can't see them.
  • Online play is optional. It connects your device directly to your opponent's, so your IP address is visible to the connection servers and to your opponent.
  • You can erase everything at any time: Settings → Delete all my data.

3. Online play (only if you choose it)

Before your first online game we show a short notice. Online games connect two devices directly using WebRTC through the PeerJS library:

  • Signaling server — by default the public PeerJS cloud server (0.peerjs.com), run by the PeerJS open-source project. It passes the connection details between the two devices.
  • STUN server — stun.l.google.com, run by Google. It tells your device its public network address.
  • TURN relay — if a direct connection isn't possible, game traffic can be relayed through PeerJS TURN servers (eu-0.turn.peerjs.com / us-0.turn.peerjs.com).

These servers receive your IP address and a random technical connection ID (for private rooms the ID includes the 4-digit room code). Their operators handle these details under their own practices; we don't receive their logs.

Your opponent's device receives your IP address (this is how direct connections work) and the game sends it: your display name and symbol, rating, avatar outfit, pet, kingdom era and stage, your moves, clock times, emotes and draw or rematch offers. We don't run a game server and we don't store your games.

In Quick match, if no player is found within about 6 seconds you play against a computer opponent, and the game tells you so ("🤖 BOT").

We use this data only to provide the online match you asked for. Tip: choose a nickname rather than your real name.

4. Invitations and sharing

WhatsApp invitations and kingdom share-cards are created on your device and sent by you through WhatsApp or your device's share menu. We don't receive the recipients' details. Invitation links contain only the room code.

5. Website, hosting and automatic updates

The game and its updates are delivered from our website, hosted by [HOSTING PROVIDER]. Like any website, the host processes technical data such as IP address, device and browser type, and request time to deliver files and keep the service secure.

While the game is open it checks a small version.json file on our website about every 20–30 minutes and installs updates at a safe moment. No usage statistics are sent with these checks.

6. Purchases and odds

The shop currently runs in test (sandbox) mode: no real money is charged and no payment details are collected. If real purchases are enabled in the Google Play version, payments are processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy, and we receive only what we need to deliver your gems (such as an order ID, the product and the time).

The chances of every Royal Spin Wheel and Royal Chest prize are shown in the game (the "Odds" button) before you spin or claim.

7. Children

The game doesn't ask for an email, phone number or real name. Online play is optional and shows other players only a nickname. If you believe a child has shared personal information through the game, contact us and we'll help remove it.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything on this device: Settings → Delete all my data, or clear the site's data / uninstall the app.
  • Avoid all connections to third-party servers: don't use the online modes.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK GDPR, Israel's Privacy Protection Law or California law) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use. Write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

9. Security and international transfers

The website and updates use HTTPS, and WebRTC game connections are encrypted (DTLS). The servers listed above may be located outside your country, for example in the European Union or the United States.

10. Changes

If we change how the game handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to play. The game currently supports:

  • Full keyboard play: arrow keys move a cursor on the board, Enter selects and moves, Esc cancels. Menus and dialogs work with Tab, Enter and Esc, and focus stays inside open dialogs.
  • Screen-reader announcements of moves, check, captures and game status, and named buttons and controls.
  • Visible focus indicators and high-contrast light text on a dark background.
  • Separate switches for sound effects and ambient music; sounds and vibration are only extra cues, never the only one.
  • The interface follows your device's "reduce motion" setting, and graphics quality can be lowered in Settings.
  • English and Hebrew interfaces, with right-to-left layout in Hebrew.

Known limitations

  • The 3D board and kingdom are drawn on a canvas. Their visual details (buildings, pets, effects) aren't described in full to screen readers; the game state is announced instead.
  • Rotating and zooming the camera needs touch gestures or a mouse; the "Center" button restores the default view.

If something blocks you from playing, please tell us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

3. משחק אונליין (רק אם בחרת בו)

לפני המשחק האונליין הראשון מוצגת הודעה קצרה. משחק אונליין מחבר שני מכשירים ישירות בטכנולוגיית WebRTC באמצעות ספריית PeerJS:

  • שרת איתות — כברירת מחדל שרת הענן הציבורי של PeerJS (0.peerjs.com), שמופעל על ידי פרויקט הקוד הפתוח PeerJS. הוא מעביר את פרטי החיבור בין שני המכשירים.
  • שרת STUN — stun.l.google.com של Google. הוא מגלה למכשיר את הכתובת הציבורית שלו ברשת.
  • ממסר TURN — אם אין אפשרות לחיבור ישיר, תעבורת המשחק יכולה לעבור דרך שרתי ה-TURN של PeerJS (eu-0.turn.peerjs.com / us-0.turn.peerjs.com).

השרתים האלה מקבלים את כתובת ה-IP שלך ומזהה חיבור טכני אקראי (בחדר פרטי המזהה כולל את קוד החדר בן 4 הספרות). המפעילים שלהם מטפלים בפרטים לפי הנהלים שלהם; אנחנו לא מקבלים את הלוגים שלהם.

המכשיר של היריב מקבל את כתובת ה-IP שלך (כך עובד חיבור ישיר), והמשחק שולח אליו: שם תצוגה וסמל, דירוג, בגדי האווטאר, חיית המחמד, העידן והשלב בממלכה, המהלכים, זמני השעון, אימוג'ים והצעות תיקו או משחק חוזר. אין לנו שרת משחקים ואנחנו לא שומרים את המשחקים שלך.

במשחק מהיר, אם לא נמצא שחקן תוך כ-6 שניות, משחקים מול יריב ממוחשב — והמשחק אומר זאת במפורש ("🤖 BOT").

אנחנו משתמשים בנתונים האלה רק כדי לקיים את המשחק האונליין שביקשת. טיפ: עדיף כינוי ולא השם האמיתי.
