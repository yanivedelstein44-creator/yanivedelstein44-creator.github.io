Privacy Policy & Accessibility

Chess of Ages: Island Realms · Last updated: November 6, 2026

The short version

  • No account, no cookies, no ads, no analytics and no tracking pixels.
  • Your islands, museum, gems, outfits, pets and settings are stored only on your device. We can't see them.
  • Games against the bots, the Time Pirates or a friend on the same device are played entirely on your device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.
  • Online with a friend is optional: only the data needed for that game (your display name, avatar look and moves) goes through Google Firebase, and only people who have your private room code can see it. No strangers, no free-text chat.
  • You can erase everything at any time: Settings → Delete all my data.

3. How games are played

The bots on the 99 islands, the Time Pirates and the residents of the Central Island are computer opponents that run on your device, and so does a game against a friend on the same device.

Online with a friend (optional). When you create or join a private room, the game uses Google Firebase (Realtime Database and Anonymous Authentication, data stored in Belgium, EU), provided by Google as our service provider. What is sent: a random anonymous user ID, the display name you chose, your avatar's outfit, pet and symbol, the room code, your color, the moves, draw and rematch requests, the fixed reactions you send (there is no free-text chat) and whether you're connected. Like any internet service, Google also processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver it.

Who can see it: only people who have the room code — the friend you invited and spectators you share the code with. There is no matchmaking with strangers, and rooms can't be listed or searched. How long: a room is deleted when its creator leaves it, or the next time the creator opens online play after an old room was left open. We don't use room data for anything else.

4. Sharing

Share images are created on your device and sent by you through your device's share menu. We don't receive the recipients' details.

5. Website, hosting and automatic updates

The game and its updates are delivered from our website, hosted by [HOSTING PROVIDER]. Like any website, the host processes technical data such as IP address, device and browser type, and request time to deliver files and keep the service secure.

While the game is open it checks a small version.json file on our website about every 20–30 minutes and installs updates at a safe moment. No usage statistics are sent with these checks.

6. Purchases and odds

Islands 1–5 are free. A single one-time purchase unlocks all 99 islands. Gems can't be bought with money: they are earned only by playing (beating bots and the Time Pirates, daily rewards and the spin wheel), and nothing that helps you win is sold.

The shop currently runs in test (sandbox) mode: no real money is charged and no payment details are collected. If the one-time purchase is enabled in the Google Play version, payment is processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy, and we receive only a confirmation that the purchase was made.

The chances of every Royal Spin Wheel and Royal Chest prize are shown in the game (the "Odds" button) before you spin or claim.

7. Children

The game doesn't ask for an email, phone number or real name. Online play only connects you with a friend you invite using a private code — there's no matchmaking with strangers and no free-text chat, only fixed reactions. If you have a question about a child's use of the game, contact us.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything on this device: Settings → Delete all my data, or clear the site's data / uninstall the app.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK GDPR, Israel's Privacy Protection Law or California law) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use. Write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. You may also complain to your data protection authority.

9. Security

The website, updates and online play use HTTPS. Online rooms are protected by server-side security rules: rooms can't be listed, only people with the code can open a room, only the two players can change the game, and only the player whose turn it is can make a move.

10. Changes

If we change how the game handles data (for example new online features), we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to play. The game currently supports:

  • Full keyboard play: arrow keys move a cursor on the board, Enter selects and moves, Esc cancels. Menus and dialogs work with Tab, Enter and Esc, and focus stays inside open dialogs. On the world map, the arrow keys move between islands.
  • Screen-reader announcements of moves, check, captures and game status, and named buttons and controls.
  • Visible focus indicators and high-contrast light text on a dark background.
  • Separate switches for sound effects and ambient music; sounds and vibration are only extra cues, never the only one.
  • The interface follows your device's "reduce motion" setting, graphics quality can be lowered in Settings, and journeys between islands can be sped up by tapping.
  • English and Hebrew interfaces, with right-to-left layout in Hebrew.

Known limitations

  • The 3D board, islands and museum are drawn on a canvas. Their visual details (monuments, pets, weather) aren't described in full to screen readers; the game state is announced instead.
  • Rotating and zooming the camera needs touch gestures or a mouse; the "Center" button restores the default view.
  • The story film in the cinema has on-screen captions but no audio narration.

If something blocks you from playing, please tell us at [CONTACT EMAIL].
